Core Standards

The Four Pillars of NOVARA

Every sketch, seam, button, and fabric swatch reflects our uncompromising dedication to material excellence.

01.

Noble Natural Fibers

We avoid synthetic polyesters and microplastics. We work exclusively with certified 280gsm Italian merino wool, 22-momme Mulberry silk, and Mongolian cashmere that feel extraordinarily soft and breathe naturally.

02.

Architectural Silhouette

Tailoring that blends formal Italian proportion with modern ease. Subtle dropped shoulders, fluid drape lines, and unconstructed soft interiors that adapt seamlessly to work, travel, and leisure.

03.

Radical Price Honesty

Traditional luxury brands mark up garments 8x to 10x over production costs to cover extravagant runway shows. By working directly with mills, NOVARA offers true runway-grade pieces at an honest 2x–3x model.

04.

Zero Plastic Packaging

Our packaging is 100% compostable and recyclable. Each garment is delivered in custom unbleached organic cotton dust pouches and soy-ink printed kraft cardboard boxes.

Client Support

Frequently Asked Questions

All in-stock orders are prepared at our Mumbai studio within 24 hours. Express courier typically delivers within 2–3 business days to metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai) and 3–5 business days to other regions across India.

We provide a 14-day complimentary exchange and return window for unworn items with original garment tags intact. Our courier will pick up the package directly from your address at zero cost.

Each product page features an exact chest, waist, and length chart. Our silhouettes are cut with modern tailored ease. For an oversized street aesthetic, select your typical size; for a razor-tailored look, consider sizing down.

Our concierge team is available Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST via email at concierge@novaralifestyle.com or phone at +91 22 4920 8800.
